    Proven in Lean

    lean/ThinIce/Mistake.lean

-- After c1-g5, Red (to move) forces three in a row within 2 Red moves.
theorem P07.c1g5_loses : ForcedWin 2 P07.red P07.afterC1G5 :=
  check_sound noHint 2 _ _ (by decide +kernel)

-- Red's winning first moves are exactly c3-e1 and d3-f1.
theorem P07.red_winning_moves :
  winningMoves 1 P07.red P07.afterC1G5 = [(16, 4), (17, 5)] := by decide +kernel

    11 of the 12 are checked by Lean’s kernel alone; P12 also trusts the compiler. Not proven: that a win is the fastest, that a move is the only one that holds (except P07), or the solved game as a whole.
