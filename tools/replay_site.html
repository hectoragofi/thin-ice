Thin Ice · a planning test for AI models

How far ahead can an AI model plan?

A small board game where every position is solved. AI models play it, and every move is checked.

1 · The game

  • 7 by 6 board, 4 stones each.
  • A stone slides until a stone or the edge stops it.
  • Three in a row wins.

2 · The test

  • 12 puzzles. In each one, Blue has a guaranteed win in 1 to 6 moves.
  • An AI model plays Blue . No code, no search program: only its own reasoning.
  • Red is a perfect opponent: it always holds out as long as possible.
  • A puzzle is lost at the first move that gives up the guaranteed win.
